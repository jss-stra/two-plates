Two Plates — Privacy

Two Plates is a personal meal-planning app. This page describes what it stores and who can see it.

What the app stores

  • Your week plans, grocery lists, food logs, water check-ins and settings.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's name and email address, used to identify your account.
  • If you use the cookbook: dishes you save, including photos you add.
  • If you join a household: your membership and the household's shared plan.
  • If you use receipts: receipt details and photos you add.

Where it is stored

The app itself is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. App data is stored in a Supabase project operated by the app owner. Sync between devices goes through that Supabase project.

Who can see it

  • A household's shared plan can be read and changed only by members of that household. Members join by email invite from the household owner.
  • Your cookbook is private to your account unless you choose to share a dish.
  • Cookbook photos load through links that expire shortly after they are issued.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no sale of personal data, no third-party analytics.
  • Nutrition figures are estimates for planning, not medical or dietary advice. Allergy and dislike filtering checks listed ingredients only; it is a planning aid, not a safety guarantee.

Deletion

You can delete cookbook dishes in the app. To remove your account or household data, open an issue on the project's public GitHub repository (jss-stra/two-plates) and the owner will delete it.

Changes

This page is updated when the app's data practices change. It reflects what the app actually does; if something here looks wrong, treat the app's behavior as the source of truth and report it via the repository.
