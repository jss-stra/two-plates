Two Plates — Terms of use

Two Plates is a personal project, provided as-is and free of charge.

Using the app

  • Use it to plan meals and share plans with your own household.
  • Only invite people you know to your household, and only upload photos and text you are allowed to share.
  • Do not try to access another household's data or disrupt the service.

No guarantees

  • The app is provided without warranties of any kind. It may change, lose data, or be discontinued.
  • Keep your own copy of anything important (the app has an export option in Settings).
  • Nutrition figures are estimates for planning only. Allergy and dislike filtering checks listed ingredients only and is not a safety guarantee; always check ingredients yourself.

Contact

Questions and problems: open an issue on the project's public GitHub repository (jss-stra/two-plates).
